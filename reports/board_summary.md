# Duka Electronics Board Summary

## Reporting Period: January–June 2026

Duka Electronics generated KES 81,391,500 in revenue from 1,000 distinct orders, with average revenue of KES 81,391.50 per order. Revenue declined from KES 15,786,500 in January to KES 10,700,000 in June, a decrease of approximately 32.2%. This trend requires investigation to establish the underlying causes.

Laptops were the leading product, contributing KES 36,125,000 (44.38%) of total revenue. Online sales generated KES 44,753,000 (54.98%), exceeding store sales. Nairobi was the strongest branch at KES 24,652,000, while Nakuru recorded the lowest branch revenue at KES 12,168,500. Rift Valley led regional revenue with a 32.53% share.

Standard customers contributed 68.06% of revenue, while Premium customers had a higher average order value of KES 95,571.69 compared with KES 76,093.41 for Standard customers.

Management should investigate the revenue decline, protect high-performing product sales, improve online performance, and assess opportunities to strengthen weaker branches. Since costs and profit margins were unavailable, these findings concern revenue rather than profitability.
